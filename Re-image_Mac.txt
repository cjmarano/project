Make sure that your Mac is turned off. If you can't shut it down normally, press and hold its power button for up to 10 seconds, until your Mac turns off. (Every Mac has a power button. On laptop computers that have Touch ID, press and hold the Touch ID button.)

Press and hold the power button again. As you continue to hold it, your Mac turns on and loads startup options. When you see “Loading startup options” or the Options icon, release the power button.

Click Options, then click the Continue button that appears beneath it.

You will see a window open labelled macOS Recovery.

Click Recovery Assistant at the top of your Mac's screen then choose Erase Mac...

Click erase and then click again to confirm.

Your MacBook will be erased and will restatrt.

Once your MacBook restarts you you'll be prompted to sign into your local Wi-Fi.

Click the Wi-Fi icon (top-right of your screen) choose your network then sign in.

When that's done the Activate Mac dialog will start. When it says 'Your Mac is activated"
click 'Exit to Recovery'.

In the Recovery window choose Reinstall (second option from the top).

Click Continue. Continue. Agree, Agree. Click the Hard Drive icon then click continue.

Your MacBook will download a fresh copy of the operating system. This may take some time
depending on your Interenet connection. Leave the MacBook connected to power and leave the lid open.

Once the install is complete the MacBook will restart and you'll be able to choose your language and Wi-Fi network.
